Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Note: This policy is a general template and does not constitute legal advice. If your school, district, or business is subject to specific data protection regulations (for example, FERPA or COPPA when handling student or minors' data), we recommend having an attorney review and adapt this document before relying on it as your official policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you contact us through our form, WhatsApp, chat, or email, including your name, email address, phone number, organization name, and the content of your message. We may also collect basic technical information (device type, browser) when you visit our site.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to your quote requests or support inquiries
  • Provide and follow up on contracted services
  • Communicate with you about your account or security incidents
  • Improve our services and this website

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This site may use basic cookies and local storage to remember preferences and to operate the chat widget. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

4. Third Parties and Service Providers

We work with outside vendors to run our business, including:

  • ThreatDown by Malwarebytes — the endpoint security platform we manage on your behalf.
  • WhatsApp Business — for direct communication with you.
  • GitHub Pages — the hosting provider for this website.
  • Our virtual chat assistant, which processes messages you send through the chat widget in order to respond to you.

These providers only receive the information necessary to deliver their service and are subject to their own privacy policies.

5. Student Data and Educational Institutions

If your organization is a school or school district, you are responsible for ensuring your use of our services complies with applicable student data protection laws (such as FERPA or COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from minors; devices we manage for educational institutions are administered under contract with the institution, not the student.

6. Security of Information

We apply reasonable security measures to protect the information we handle, including the same cybersecurity tools we offer our clients. No system is completely infallible; if we identify an incident affecting your data, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

7. Your Rights

You may request at any time: access to your information, correction of inaccurate data, deletion of your information, or opt out of certain uses of your data. Contact us using the information below to make a request.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as an active service relationship exists, plus any additional time needed to meet subsequent legal, tax, or support obligations.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page with a new "last updated" date.

10. Contact for Privacy Questions

If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise any of your rights, email us at unlockmxpro@gmail.com.
